Our Journey

❧

Kalinga Handicraft was born from a simple belief — that Indian handmade art is not just beautiful, but also meaningful and sustainable.

  • 2018 – The Beginning

    Started as a small initiative to support local artisans and bring their incredible work to a wider audience.

  • 2020 – Growing Together

    Partnered with artisan communities across India, expanding our collection of authentic crafts.

  • 2022 – Building Impact

    Empowered more than 500+ artisans and shipped handmade love to thousands of happy homes.

  • 2024 & Beyond

    Continuing our journey to preserve heritage, inspire future generations and create a positive impact.
